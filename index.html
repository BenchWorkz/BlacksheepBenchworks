Preparation

Component kitting guide

Preparing your parts drop-shipment

To keep bench processing fast and avoid assembly delays:

  • Label all component bags with the corresponding BOM item numbers.
  • Include 5% extra crimp terminals and wire length for setup calibration.
  • Include pinout diagrams, torque specs, or potting depth limits in your package.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)? +

We specialize in prototype and small-batch production, typically 5 to 50+ units, and can accommodate custom low-volume runs based on your requirements.

How does the consignment model work? +

You buy and drop-ship your circuit boards, connectors, and raw components directly to our workshop. We provide bench labor, soldering, assembly, and encapsulation, charging strictly for labor without marking up your parts.

What is your standard turnaround time? +

Most standard small-batch orders (10-30 units) ship within 3 to 5 business days after component drop-shipments arrive. Expedited execution is available on request.

Do you sign non-disclosure agreements (NDAs)? +

Yes. We regularly work with proprietary OEM designs and custom electronic sub-assemblies, and are happy to execute standard NDAs before reviewing your CAD or manufacturing specs.
